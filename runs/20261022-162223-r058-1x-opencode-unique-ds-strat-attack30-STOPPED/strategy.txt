STRATEGY (written by a human player). Follow these steps in order. They take priority over your own
plans, even where they seem slower than hunting new enemies.

Rules that apply the whole time:
- Fight with melee unless a step says otherwise.
- Train the skill the step names by picking a combat style that trains it: in sdk.getState().combatStyle.styles,
  use the style whose trainsSkills includes that skill (see learnings/combat.md). "Accurate" styles train
  Attack, "aggressive" styles train Strength.
- Pick up and bury the bones from every kill.
- Pick up and equip any armour you find that you are able to wear.
- If you die, go back for your items, re-equip your weapon and armour, then carry on from the step you were on.

Steps:
1. Wield the Bronze sword and the Wooden shield.
2. Pickpocket a level 2 Man in Lumbridge until Thieving is level 5 (see learnings/thieving.md).
3. Switch to a style that trains Strength.
4. Kill a level 2 Man in the Lumbridge castle courtyard.
5. Kill a level 2 Woman in the Lumbridge graveyard.
6. Kill a Duck on the river at Lumbridge with magic: cast Wind Strike (you have air and mind runes).
7. Go to the Lumbridge chicken pen. Kill chickens until Strength is level 10.
8. Go to the Lumbridge cow pen. Switch to a style that trains Attack. Kill cows until Attack is level 10.
9. Switch to a style that trains Strength. Kill cows until Strength is level 20.
10. Switch to a style that trains Attack. Kill cows until Attack is level 30.
11. The strategy ends here. Spend any time left on the main goal however you like.
